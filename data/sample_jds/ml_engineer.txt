Machine Learning Engineer (Entry Level)

About the role
We are looking for an early-career ML engineer to help build and ship models
that power our product. You will work across the stack, from data preparation to
serving models in production.

Required qualifications
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals and scikit-learn.
- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Comfortable with SQL for data extraction.
- Experience building or consuming REST APIs.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience deploying models with Docker.
- Familiarity with a cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Exposure to MLOps tools and model monitoring.

Responsibilities
- Prepare datasets, train and evaluate models, and iterate on model quality.
- Package models behind APIs and collaborate with backend engineers.
- Write clean, tested, maintainable Python code.
